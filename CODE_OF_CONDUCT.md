# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be constructive and respectful. Technical criticism of ideas is welcome;
attacks on people are not.

Expected of everyone:

- Critique code, designs and claims — not the person behind them.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Assume good faith across languages, cultures and time zones; many
  participants work in a second (or third) language.
- Keep discussions on topic; take tangents elsewhere.

## Scope

This applies to this repository and its spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and to any official project channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock discussions, or ban accounts
for violations.

- Report violations by opening a maintainer ping in an issue, or — for
  sensitive matters — through GitHub's private contact channels on this
  repository.
- Reports are handled by the maintainers; unresolved or severe cases may
  be escalated to GitHub staff.

Instances of abusive behavior automatically become grounds for removal
from project spaces.
